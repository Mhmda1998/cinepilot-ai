# CinePilot AI - Pitch Deck

## The Problem

Film production teams spend days manually analyzing screenplays for props, wardrobe, sound, lighting, budget, casting, and schedules.

## The Solution

CinePilot AI transforms screenplay text into a complete Production Bible in seconds.

## Revolutionary Features

1. Production Bible - Unified report of all analyses
2. Script Coverage Report - Hollywood-style analysis
3. Scene Mood Analyzer - Mood, music, and color suggestions
4. Dialogue Emotion Analyzer - Understands character emotions
5. Camera Angle Suggestor - Professional shot recommendations
6. Shooting Schedule - Automatic shooting day grouping
7. Budget Estimator - Automatic cost calculation
8. AI Casting Suggestions - Actor recommendations
9. Continuity Tracker - Detects continuity errors
10. AI Storyboard Generator - Visual storyboards from scenes
11. AI Scene Image Generator - Cinematic images per scene
12. ClickHouse Cloud - Real production data storage
